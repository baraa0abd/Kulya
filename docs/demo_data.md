# Demo data

Everything here is invented. No real patient, name, or record is used; every `Patient` row has
`is_synthetic=true`. Source: `backend/seed/data/demo.json`, generated by
`backend/seed/data/build_demo_data.py` (edit the builder, then re-run it).

## Loading

```bash
cd backend
DEMO_PASSWORD=<choose one> python manage.py seed_demo --approve-facts
```

- Idempotent: running it again creates nothing new.
- `--approve-facts` records each note's hand-written expected extraction as a draft reviewed by
  `nurse1` and merges it into `SessionFacts`, so the timeline and flags are populated with no LLM.
  Without the flag you get patients, sessions, and notes only (no drafts, no facts, so only the
  vitals-based flags R1, R3, R4).
- Thresholds are demo placeholders, not clinical guidance (`docs/clinical_disclaimer.md`).

## Users

| Username | Role | Sees |

| `nurse1` | nurse | all three patients; creates sessions and notes; reviews drafts |

## Schedule

15 sessions per patient, Saturday / Monday / Wednesday, 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-30. 45 sessions, 46 notes.

## Storylines and expected flags

### SYN-001 — recurring chills with blood-pressure drops (demo centrepiece)

AVF, morning shift. Sessions 1–9 are quiet; a first mention of feeling cold in session 8. From
session 10 the notes describe chills and dizziness and the typed vitals show larger systolic drops.

| Flag | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `R1_INTRA_SBP_DROP` ×4 | sessions 10, 11, 13, 15 | pre − min intra SBP = 30, 27, 45, 40 mmHg (threshold 20) |
| `R2_RECURRING_SYMPTOM` ×1 | on session 15 | `chills` in sessions 11, 13, 15 = 3 of the last 5 |

Worth showing: session 13's note mentions "٩٥ على ٦٠" and "١٠٠ مل" — they appear under *mentioned
values* and are never copied into vitals. Session 6 ("ما عدها رجفة ولا دوخة") is a negation and
yields no symptom. Dizziness appears in sessions 10 and 15 only, so it does **not** trigger R2.

### SYN-002 — access bleeding and one large weight gain

AVF, morning shift. Stable pressures throughout.

| Flag | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `R4_INTERDIALYTIC_GAIN` ×1 | session 9 | 74.0 kg after session 8 → 77.6 kg before session 9 = 3.6 kg (threshold 3.0) |
| `R5_RECURRING_ACCESS_ISSUE` ×1 | on session 15 | `bleeding` in sessions 13 and 15 = 2 of the last 3 |

Worth showing: session 10 has two notes — the nurse first wrote that a headache happened during the
session, then a correction note that supersedes it. The original text is untouched; its draft is
rejected and only the correction's facts are in the record. Session 14 has `poor_flow` once (no flag).
Sessions 3, 7, 12 have no intradialytic minimum, exercising the post-SBP fallback without flagging.

### SYN-003 — stable, with two incomplete records

CVC, afternoon shift. No symptoms.

| Flag | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `R3_MISSING_REQUIRED` ×1 | session 4 | post-session weight not recorded (the note says the patient left in a hurry) |
| `R3_MISSING_REQUIRED` ×1 | session 11 | post-session systolic and diastolic not recorded (the note says the monitor failed) |

No other rule fires for this patient: that is the "quiet patient stays quiet" check.

## Not in the seed

- `R6_PENDING_REVIEW` (draft pending > 24 h) and the "no note within 24 h" half of R3 depend on the
  clock, so the seed contains no such case. Both are covered by unit tests.
- With `--approve-facts` there are no pending drafts. To demo the review screen, post a new note as
  `nurse1`.

## Golden set for AI evaluation

`eval/golden/golden.json`: 20 additional synthetic notes with hand-written expected extractions,
tagged by difficulty (negation, past events, spelling variants, Arabic-Indic digits, access
findings, uncertain statements). Run `python ../eval/run_eval.py --client mock|anthropic` from
`backend/`.
